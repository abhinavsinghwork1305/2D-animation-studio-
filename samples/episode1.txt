SCENE 1: The Lost Key

Once upon a time, a young girl named Maya woke up to find her favourite red bicycle missing from the garden.
She searched everywhere — behind the bushes, inside the shed, even under the old oak tree.


SCENE 2: Clues in the Meadow

Her neighbour, an old tortoise named Gerald, spotted tyre tracks leading across the meadow.
Together they followed the trail through the tall golden grass, past a row of bright sunflowers.
The morning air smelled of dew and adventure.


SCENE 3: The Friendly Thief

At the edge of the meadow they found a family of rabbits who had borrowed the bicycle to carry their baby carrots home.
Maya laughed and helped them unload the basket.
Gerald nodded slowly and said that kindness always finds its way back.


SCENE 4: Home Before Sunset

Maya rode her bicycle back along the golden path as the sun dipped low and painted the sky pink and orange.
She waved goodbye to Gerald and the rabbit family.
That evening she wrote in her diary — today I lost something small and found something wonderful.


SCENE 5: The Dream

That night Maya dreamed of flying over the meadow on her red bicycle.
The stars twinkled like old friends.
She smiled in her sleep, and somewhere far away Gerald smiled too.
The end.
