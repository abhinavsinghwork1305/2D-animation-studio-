SCENE 1: The Storm Cloud

A tiny rain cloud named Nimbus floated sadly above the village because nobody wanted rain on a sunny day.
All the children ran inside whenever Nimbus appeared.
Nimbus felt very alone and very misunderstood.


SCENE 2: The Wilting Garden

Down below, Farmer Chen stared at his wilting vegetable garden.
The crops were dry and cracked and desperately needed water.
He looked up at the sky and sighed a long and weary sigh.


SCENE 3: A Surprising Friendship

Nimbus drifted down and saw the garden for the first time.
Something wonderful stirred inside the little cloud.
With a happy shake Nimbus released a gentle shower of warm summer rain.
Farmer Chen cheered and danced between the rows of vegetables.


SCENE 4: The Rainbow

As the rain stopped, sunlight broke through and painted a bright rainbow across the sky.
The children rushed outside laughing and pointing at the colours.
Nimbus glowed with pride at the edge of the rainbow.


SCENE 5: Always Welcome

From that day on the village always welcomed Nimbus with open arms.
Nimbus learned that being different is not something to be sad about.
Every garden needs rain and every sky needs a cloud.
The end.
