SCENE 1: The Brave Little Star

High above the sleeping world a tiny star named Pip burned with excitement every single night.
All the other stars were old and calm and steady but Pip loved to zoom and spin and leap across the sky.


SCENE 2: The Falling Night

One evening Pip zoomed too fast and tumbled down through the clouds.
Down and down Pip fell, faster and faster, the wind rushing past like a roar.


SCENE 3: Soft Landing

Pip landed softly in the middle of a quiet forest clearing.
Two children, Leo and Zara, were camping there and saw the whole amazing adventure.
They ran over and found a small glowing pebble pulsing with warm golden light.


SCENE 4: The Wish

Leo and Zara held the glowing pebble gently between them.
They made a wish together — that Pip could fly home safely.
A beam of golden light shot upward from the pebble and arched across the whole dark sky.


SCENE 5: Back Among the Stars

Pip followed the golden beam all the way back up to the stars.
From high above Pip twinkled three times — a secret thank you just for Leo and Zara.
They laughed and cheered and fell asleep smiling under the starry sky.
The end.
